fix(ir): release a comparison's fresh refcounted operand, and record the plan

Stage 6's valgrind leg found it: `$key === "bad"` allocated the literal and
nothing ever freed it, because a comparison reads its operands without taking
ownership and no durable slot held the fresh one. The `Concat` arm has applied
exactly this rule since it landed; the scalar-operator arm needed it only once
string comparison started lowering at all.

`examples/errors.mwl` is now valgrind-clean under WSL against a Linux build,
alongside `throw`/`trace`/`arrays`/`hello`/`calls`/`arith`.

Also: the plan's status block, for the fields this loop changed.
