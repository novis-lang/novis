Novis is in active development. Part of the standard library is implemented and tested;
the HTTP server, the database layer and the capability system are specified but not
built. There is one implementation, no independent security audit yet, and no production
deployment anywhere. PHP and Node.js have decades of battle-hardening — the bugs they
still have are at least well-known bugs. Choosing Novis today means choosing a language
you can watch being built, not one you can bet a product on.
