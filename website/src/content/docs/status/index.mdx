## Should you use it?

Not for anything real, not yet. There is no release, no stable syntax and no
dependency story. **The useful question today is whether to follow the project,
not whether to adopt it.**

If you are evaluating it for a future project, the two things to watch are the
package story and the first-party framework — those, rather than more language
features, are what stand between Novis and being usable.
