Novis is a young programming language. It combines ideas from other languages with ideas of our
own, and we develop them together to get the best of all worlds.

Even though Novis is new, and is currently built by a minimal team, it still takes a lot
of resources. The biggest one is time. Novis is open source and free, and it always will
be. But getting all of this running, and keeping it running, takes a lot of time. Time
is exactly what we are willing to invest to reach our goal.

But there are also things that already cost real money. Servers, licences, hardware…
Novis already has a certain baseline that costs money every month.

## This is where sponsors come in. Maybe you?!

Sponsors help us pay our fixed costs. Right now we are not a company, just individuals
trying to build something great. Our plan for the future is set out already, though.
When the time comes, we will found a *gemeinnütziger Verein* in Austria. That is a charitable
association, the best legal form for this project.

A *g. V.* is a non-profit organisation whose purpose is not to make money. We still need
that legal shape to comply with the law, and at the same time it makes us look serious
from the outside. So at some point we will be a non-profit organisation with transparent
financial reports. We will also have to employ people, and we will need offices. All of that
costs money, and it has to come from somewhere to keep the machine running.

Before we go and found an association, though, we should concentrate on the essentials
first. Sponsored money will be published transparently. We will use the
[Open Source Collective](https://opencollective.com/) for that.

## So you can support us today

Every donation helps us, no matter how large, how small, how long or how short. Today
that money is received by a private individual: Roland, the creator of Novis. There is

Good. Now you know how we are set up and why we need money.
