Novis is a young programming language. It combines ideas from other languages with ideas of our
own, and we develop them together to get the best of all worlds.

Even though Novis is new, and is currently built by a minimal team, it still takes a lot
of resources. The biggest one is time. Novis is open source and free, and it always will
be. But getting all of this running, and keeping it running, takes a lot of time. Time
is exactly what we are willing to invest to reach our goal.

But there are also things that already cost real money. Servers, licences, hardware…
Novis already has a certain baseline that costs money every month.

## This is where sponsors come in. Maybe you?!

Sponsors help us pay our fixed costs. Novis is already run by a company. It is a sole
proprietorship (*Einzelunternehmen*) in Austria: a business that one person owns and runs.
It is the simplest and cheapest form of a company in Austria.

Our plan for the future is set out already. When the team is bigger and sponsoring makes
it possible, we will found a *FlexCo* with the name Novis-Lang. A FlexCo (*Flexible
Kapitalgesellschaft*) is a form of company that Austria introduced in 2024. It was made
for startups and young, growing companies. With it we can employ people and rent offices.
All of that costs money, and the money has to come from somewhere.

## Where the money goes

Sponsor money will go through the [Open Source Collective](https://opencollective.com/)
(OSC). The OSC is a nonprofit that keeps the money for open-source projects. It shows
every payment in and every payment out on a public page. Everybody can see how much
sponsors give and what we spend it on.

The OSC keeps 10% of each donation as its fee. We pay our costs from the rest. For each
cost, we send the OSC an invoice or a receipt, and it pays us. These invoices are public
on the same page.

## What does not change

The company can change its form. These promises stay the same:

- Novis is open source, and it always will be.
- Novis is under the MIT license, and it always will be.
- The open-source code is all of Novis. There is no paid edition with extra features.
- We do our best to keep Novis the best and simplest way to run the web applications of
  today and of the future.

In the future, we may sell services for Novis, for example consulting. A service never
takes a feature away from the free Novis.
