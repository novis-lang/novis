Novis is an independent, modern programming language for web servers and the command line. It
is open source and free to use. It is under the MIT license, and it always will be.

A solid language, runtime and set of tools needs time. It also needs infrastructure that costs
money every month. Novis is tested on several processor architectures, and it needs servers,
domains and developer tools.

Sponsors pay for this work. Your money keeps Novis stable, maintained and independent.

## How you can give

Choose the way that suits you. You can give €5 once, or support Novis as a company every month.

## Tiers

Your tier depends on how much you give per month. It is the same on every channel. A gift you
make once counts for 12 months. We divide it by 12, so €300 once is the same as €25 per month
for one year.

<Sponsors show="tiers" />

By default, we publish nothing about you: not your name, not your logo and not your amount.
You are only counted in the number of donors and in the total. If you ask us to, we show your
name, your logo, your link or your amount. Tell us when you give. You can change your mind at
any time. The [privacy policy](/datenschutz/) has the details.

## Where the money goes

All money, after the fees of the platforms, goes into Novis:

- **Development:** time for the speed of the compiler, for language features and for the
  documentation.
- **Infrastructure:** the machines that build and test Novis, hardware for benchmarks, and
  test environments.
- **Ecosystem:** official libraries and the tools for code editors.

Every quarter, we publish a short report. It shows what Novis reached in that quarter and what
comes next. It also shows how many people and companies gave, the total after the fees of the
platforms, and what we spent it on. It does not show who gave how much.

## What does not change

Novis can grow, and its company can change its form. These promises stay the same:

- Novis is open source under the MIT license, and it always will be.
- The open-source release is all of Novis. There are no paid features, no extra syntax for
  companies and no hidden parts of the core.
- Everybody can learn Novis, build with it and deploy it for free.

Thank you for helping to build Novis!
