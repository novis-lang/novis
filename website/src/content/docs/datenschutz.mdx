## What this website does with your data

This website is a static documentation website. It sets **no cookies**. It uses **no
tracking or analytics services**. It has **no user accounts**. The search and the filters
run completely in your browser. They send no data to us.

Your choice of a light or a dark theme is stored only in your browser (local storage). It
is not sent to us.

## Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly

When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it to keep its service
secure. GitHub does this for every visitor. We cannot see these logs. The legal basis is

GitHub decides how long it keeps this data. GitHub transfers data to the United States
under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses. The
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
explains what GitHub collects and how long it keeps it.

## Donations and sponsors

You can give money through GitHub Sponsors, Polar or a bank transfer.
The platform or your bank processes the payment, and its own privacy policy applies. Some
platforms show donors on their own pages, depending on your settings there. We do not
control that.

From the platform or the bank transfer, we receive the data we need to handle your gift.
This is usually your name or user name, your e-mail address, the amount and the date. We
use this data to handle the donation, to send you an invoice if you ask for one, and for
our accounting. The legal basis is the handling of your donation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and
our legal duty to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Austrian tax law requires
us to keep these records for seven years (§ 132 BAO). After that, we delete them.

**By default, we publish nothing about you.** Your gift is only counted in the number of
donors and in the total of our quarterly report. Neither shows who you are or how much you
gave.

We publish your name, your logo, your link or your amount on this website only if you
explicitly ask us to. You choose each of these on its own. The legal basis is your consent
(Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to the e-mail
address above. We then remove the data from the website with the next update. The
withdrawal does not make the earlier publication unlawful.

Copies of the website that other services made before the withdrawal, for example search
engines or web archives, are outside our control.

## Links to other services

This website links to other services (GitHub, Discord and the donation platforms). When you
click such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies. This website does not embed
content from these services. No data is sent to them before you click.

restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21).
To use these rights, write to the e-mail address above.

to the Austrian Data Protection Authority:
