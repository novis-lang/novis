## What this website does with your data

This website is a static documentation website. It sets **no cookies**. It has **no user
accounts**. The search and the filters run completely in your browser. They send no data to
us.

We count visits with Matomo, on a server that we run ourselves. The section [Visitor statistics](#visitor-statistics) explains what we count, and
how you can turn it off.

Your choice of a light or a dark theme is stored only in your browser (local storage). It
is not sent to us.

## Visitor statistics

We want to know which pages people read, so we can improve the documentation. For this, we
use [Matomo](https://matomo.org/), an open-source analytics program. Matomo runs on our own
server at `stats.novis-lang.org`.

We rent this server from Contabo GmbH, Welfenstraße 22, 81541 München, Germany. The server
is in the European Union. Contabo stores the data for us and processes it only on our
instructions. We have a data processing agreement with Contabo (Art. 28 GDPR). No data goes
to Matomo the company or to any other third party.

**What we count.** When you open a page, your browser sends us one request with:

- the address of the page, without anything after a `?` or a `#`,
- the title of the page,
- the website you came from, only its address (for example `https://www.google.com`).

The server also receives what every web server receives: your IP address, your browser and
operating system (the user agent), your preferred language and the time. Matomo removes the
last two parts of your IP address (for example `192.0.2.17` becomes `192.0.0.0`) before it
stores anything. From the shortened address, Matomo finds your country. Your full IP
address is never stored.

**What we do not do.** We set no cookies, and we store nothing in your browser for the
statistics. We do not create a profile of you. We do not combine this data with other data.
Matomo groups the requests of one visit with a value calculated from the shortened IP
address and the browser data. This value changes every day, so we cannot recognize you
on the next day. We cannot see who you are, and we do not try to find out.

**How long we keep it.** Matomo deletes the data of single visits after 90 days. After
that, we keep only totals, for example "this page had 300 views in May". Totals do not
contain any data about you.

**Legal basis.** Our legitimate interest in improving this website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**How to turn it off.** You can object at any time with the switch below. If you turn it
off, your browser stores `nv-stats-opt-out` in its local storage, and sends no more
requests to the statistics server. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global
Privacy Control" signal, this website never counts your visits. If JavaScript is turned
off, this website never counts your visits.

When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it to keep its service

## Donations and sponsors

You can give money through GitHub Sponsors, Polar or a bank transfer.
The platform or your bank processes the payment, and its own privacy policy applies. Some
platforms show donors on their own pages, depending on your settings there. We do not
control that.

From the platform or the bank transfer, we receive the data we need to handle your gift.
This is usually your name or user name, your e-mail address, the amount and the date. We
use this data to handle the donation, to send you an invoice if you ask for one, and for
our accounting. The legal basis is the handling of your donation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and
our legal duty to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Austrian tax law requires
us to keep these records for seven years (§ 132 BAO). After that, we delete them.

**By default, we publish nothing about you.** Your gift is only counted in the number of
donors and in the total of our quarterly report. Neither shows who you are or how much you
gave.

We publish your name, your logo, your link or your amount on this website only if you
explicitly ask us to. You choose each of these on its own. The legal basis is your consent
(Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to the e-mail
address above. We then remove the data from the website with the next update. The
withdrawal does not make the earlier publication unlawful.

Copies of the website that other services made before the withdrawal, for example search
engines or web archives, are outside our control.

This website links to other services (GitHub, Discord and the donation platforms). When you

content from these services. No data is sent to them before you click.
