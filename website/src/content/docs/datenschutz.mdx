## What this website does with your data

This website is a static documentation website. It sets **no cookies**. It uses **no
tracking or analytics services**. It has **no user accounts**. The search and the filters
run completely in your browser. They send no data to us.

Your choice of a light or a dark theme is stored only in your browser (local storage). It
is not sent to us.

## Access data (server logs)

When you open this website, the hosting provider processes the data that it needs to
deliver the website. This data is your IP address, the date and time, the page you opened
and the user agent of your browser. The purpose is to deliver the website and to keep it
secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The logs are deleted after **14 days** at the latest.

## Links to other services

This website links to other services (GitHub, Discord). When you click such a link, the

services. No data is sent to them before you click.
