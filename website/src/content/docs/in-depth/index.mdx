These pages explain why Novis works the way it does. You do not need them to write Novis code.
Read them when you want to know the reasons behind a design.

Each page answers one question:

- [What Novis is for](/in-depth/what-for/): the programs Novis is made to run.
- [What Novis will never be](/in-depth/never/): the things Novis does not do, and will not add.
- [How we decide](/in-depth/how-we-decide/): the five priorities, in the order we use them.
- [Design principles](/in-depth/design-principles/): the ideas you meet on every page of the syntax.
- [Concepts](/in-depth/concepts/): five ideas explained one at a time, such as tainted input and limits.
- [Where Novis falls behind](/in-depth/falls-behind/): the costs of these choices, stated plainly.
- [Roadmap](/in-depth/roadmap/): what is built, and what comes next.
- [What changed](/in-depth/what-changed/): the changes in each release of Novis.

Some pages link to a decision record on GitHub. A decision record is the full reasoning behind one
design choice. It is written for people who work on Novis, so it uses more technical words.
