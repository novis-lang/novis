## Two things worth knowing before you read further

**Novis is pre-alpha.** Some of what is documented here is decided but not yet
built, and some syntax is still provisional. Every page says so where it
applies, and the [status page](../status/) is generated from the plan the team
works against.

**Every page links to its reasoning.** Novis keeps a written record of every
decision that would be expensive to reverse — what was chosen, what was
rejected, and why. Those records are [published in full](../design/). If a guide
tells you *what* and you want *why*, the link at the bottom of the page goes
straight there.
