# ADR 0222 — the loop's live goal is a committed field of `data/chain.json`, and a goal's floor is a view over the goals it has walked

- **Scope:** where the loop's live goal is kept, how the driver moves it, and what a goal's floor is. It changes
  nothing a Novis program or the `nvs` binary does, and nothing about what a check runs.
- **Depends on:** [0221](0221.md).
- **Validated by:** `tools/nv/test/chain.test.ts` for the view and the pointer, `bun nv chain --check` and `bun nv check`
  for the record, and a rehearsed run in which a reached goal made the next one live in its own commit.

> **In short:** `data/chain.json` is `{ "live": <slug>, "goals": [<slug>, ...] }`. The driver's goal switch
> moves `live` and commits that file alone. The plan the driver runs for a goal is its record with every
> check of every goal in front of it carried in as one `floor` stage, so a switch copies nothing and a
> walked goal keeps its checks.

## Context

Goal `tooling-overhaul`'s data model kept the live goal in the git-ignored `.loop/state.sqlite`. A fresh
clone has no such file, so it had no live goal: `bun nv orient` and `bun nv loop --owed` exited 2 there,
and the `pre-push` hook, which asks `--owed`, refused every push. CI's `bun nv plan --check` computed the
walked goals with no live goal at all. Nothing wrote the pointer after the cutover either, so a reached
goal could not hand over to the next one.

The same goal described the floor as a view over every walked goal, and the view was never built: the live
goal's floor existed only as copies inside its own record, and the goals behind it carried none.

## Decision

### 1. The live goal is a record field

`data/chain.json` holds `live` beside `goals`. `live` is a reference to a goal record, and the chain's own
check requires it to be on `goals`. It is the one answer to "which goal is live" on every clone and in CI,
by `rule:tooling/a-repository-fact-is-one-json-record`. `.loop/` keeps what is per machine: the memo, the
run's counts and the gates' standing verdicts.

### 2. The driver moves it

When a goal's sweep with the floor gate open, its WSL leg, its valgrind sweep and its two goal-end gates are
green, `bun nv loop` makes the next goal on `goals` live, commits `data/chain.json` alone under a subject
that names both goals, brings up the new goal's services and goes on. After the last goal the chain is
complete and the run ends. `bun nv chain`'s edits change `goals` and never `live`.

### 3. The floor is a view

A goal's plan is `goalPlan`: its record, with every check of every goal in front of it on `goals` carried
in under the stage the record titles `floor`, or a stage added for it. A check is carried once by id, and
once more by what it asks, apart from its id and stage. The walked goals' fixtures and valgrind skips come
with their checks. A goal that has walked keeps its checks, since the view reads them.

## Consequences

- A fresh clone, CI and the `pre-push` hook see the live goal the driver works on.
- The goal switch is one commit of one file, and it can be read in `git log` by the goals it names.
- A goal record behind the live one no longer needs its floor written into it. The records that already
  carry one keep it, and the view carries a check once however many records hold it.
- A person can move the live goal by editing one field, which is the same edit the driver makes. The chain's
  check still refuses a `live` that is not on the list.
