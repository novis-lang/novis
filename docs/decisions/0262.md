# ADR 0262 — the goal switch deletes the goal it leaves, and no goal's checks are carried as a floor

- **Scope:** what the driver's goal switch does to the goal it leaves, and what a goal's plan holds
  besides its own checks. Which goal is live, and how the chain is ordered, are [0222](0222.md)'s and
  unchanged. The one-time deletion of the goals walked before `goal-closeout` is that goal's Stage 6,
  not this rule.
- **Depends on:** [0222](0222.md) for the chain record and the floor as a view, [0225](0225.md) for the
  graduated floor checks, and [0226](0226.md) for the selection store that keys the sweep.
- **Validated by:** `tools/nv/test/switch.test.ts` and `tools/nv/test/chain.test.ts`.

> **In short:** when a goal is reached, the switch deletes its prose, its record and its handoff record,
> removes its slug from `data/chain.json`'s `goals`, and commits that with the move of `live`. No goal
> is ever in front of the live one, so a goal's plan is its own record and nothing else. The permanent
> suites and `bun nv verify` protect finished work. `goal-closeout` is the first goal this rule covers;
> no goal before it was ever deleted by the rule.

## Context

[0222](0222.md) made a goal's floor a view: the plan the driver ran for a goal was its record with every
check of every walked goal carried in. [0225](0225.md) graduated the walked test checks the permanent
suite already ran, because the floor had grown to hundreds of checks that mostly re-ran `cargo test`
and the case trees. What was left was machinery kept for a guarantee the suites already gave: a walked
goal's prose, record and handoff stayed on disk forever, every one of them a document that cites code
and drifts from it, and the chain's `--check`, the owner gate, the side-goal plan and the sweep each
carried a branch for walked and retired goals.

The audit in `goal-closeout`'s Stage 3 read every walked goal and moved what each one had decided into
a rule, a record or a module doc, and every check that proved more than a suite into a test. After
that, a walked goal holds nothing a later session reads.

## Decision

### 1. The switch deletes the goal it leaves

Once a goal's sweep, its Linux legs and its goal-end gates are green, the switch deletes
`docs/agent/goals/<slug>.md`, `data/goals/<slug>.json` and `data/goals/<slug>.handoff.json`, removes
the slug from `goals`, sets `live` to the next goal, renders the goal plan and commits all of it as one
commit, ``docs(loop): the chain advances from `a` to `b` ``, whose body says the goal was deleted.
Nothing is archived. `git log` is the history.

### 2. No floor

A goal's plan is its own record. The live goal is always first on the chain, so no check is carried in
front of it, and a side goal's plan is its own record too. The floor stage, the walked-goal set, the
graduation of walked test checks and the retired goal with an empty check list all go, with their
tests. The suites, `bun nv verify` and CI are the safety net for finished work.

### 3. A decision moves out before the goal is reached

A goal's prose is deleted with it, so before a session writes `DONE` it moves any decision only that
prose holds to its home: a rule, a record or a module doc. A goal's own prose and handoff are the only
documents that may name it, and `bun nv chain --check` fails on any other prose that names a goal not
on the chain. Decision records and `docs/agent/goal-decisions.md` are frozen history and exempt.

### 4. From `goal-closeout` on

`goal-closeout` is the first goal the rule covers. The goals walked before it were never deleted by the
rule. Its own Stage 6 deletes them once, by hand, and the rule then holds for every goal after it.

## Consequences

The tree loses one prose file, one record and one handoff per walked goal, and the tools lose every
branch that told a walked goal from a live one. A regression in finished work is caught by the suite
that tests it, or by nothing. A check that proved more than a suite has to become a test before its
goal is reached, because the check goes with the goal.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep the floor and delete only the prose.** The records would still have to stay current against
  code that moves on, which is the drift this record removes.
- **Archive walked goals under another directory.** An archive is a document nobody reads that still
  matches every search. `git log` already holds it.
