- **A gap written only as a record under `data/gaps/` is deleted by the next `nv import --write`.** The importer rebuilds `data/gaps/` from the `//! # Known gaps` blocks alone, removes every record it did not produce, and ignores a `/// # Known gaps` on a function. So a new gap goes into its module's `//!` block with a bold title and a `— owner:` line, and its record is the one `readGaps` (`tools/nv/import/gaps.ts`) derives, slug included.
