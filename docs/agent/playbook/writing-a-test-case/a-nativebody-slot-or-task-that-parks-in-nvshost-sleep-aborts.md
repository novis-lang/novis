- **A `NativeBody` slot or task that parks in `nvs_host::sleep` aborts the whole test binary when its group cancels it.** The forced unwind crosses `crates/nvs-runtime/src/closure.rs`'s `invoke_native` `extern "C"` frame, and the process stops with `STATUS_STACK_BUFFER_OVERRUN` and no panic message of its own. Let a native test closure park only where nothing cancels it, and stop siblings while they sit in Rust frames instead. [until: gone crates/nvs-runtime/src/closure.rs:invoke_native]
