# M17 — The image component's second wave (not yet sized)

**No goal on the chain carries it.** The image component, `Novis\Pdf` and `Novis\Spreadsheet` are built
into the `nvs` binary (`rule:packaging/the-first-party-components-are-built-in`), always on, under
`Novis\`. What is left is the image component's correctness and animation, which
`rule:core-classes/image-format-roster`, `rule:core-classes/image-correct-by-default` and
`rule:core-classes/image-pixel-model` place and contract.

**Scope.** AVIF and JPEG XL honour their orientation and convert to sRGB as the other formats do
(`rule:core-classes/image-correct-by-default`), and `Image::info` fills `exif`
(`rule:core-classes/image-pixel-model`). Animated images — APNG and the other animated formats the roster
names — decode their frames, and `frames` is the real count (`rule:core-classes/image-format-roster`).
`rule:core-api/tier-roster` lists the component's roster with the rest of the tiers.

**Verify:** an AVIF and a JPEG XL fixture with an orientation tag and an embedded profile decode upright
and in sRGB, `Image::info` returns the `exif` fields a fixture carries, and an animated fixture of each
animated format decodes every frame with `frames` equal to its count.
