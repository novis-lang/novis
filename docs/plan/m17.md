# M17 — Document components: the image second wave, `Novis\Pdf` and `Novis\Spreadsheet` (not yet sized)

**Backlog 6.** The goal `pdf` carried Part 2, and built the first half of Part 1; the rest of Part 1 is
unscheduled, and the goal `spreadsheet` carries Part 3. Its one
precondition is M9, which is done: the image component and the extension system that loads, caps and
pins a component are on disk. Every part is a first-party component built into the `nvs` binary
(`rule:packaging/the-first-party-components-are-built-in`), always on, under `Novis\`.

**The line against M9.** M9 builds the image component's first wave as the extension system's proof, and
keeps it. This milestone is everything `rule:core-classes/image-format-roster`, `rule:core-classes/pdf-render-has-no-io`,
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-has-no-io` and `rule:core-classes/pdf-page-is-an-image-source` placed and contracted but left unscheduled:
three parts, each large enough to be one or more goals of its own.

**Part 1 — the image component's second wave.** What is left of it is correctness and animation. AVIF
and JPEG XL honour their orientation and convert to sRGB as the other formats do
(`rule:core-classes/image-correct-by-default`), and `Image::info` fills `exif`
(`rule:core-classes/image-pixel-model`). Animated images — APNG and the other animated formats the roster
names — decode their frames, and `frames` is the real count (`rule:core-classes/image-format-roster`).

**Part 2 — `Novis\Pdf`.** HTML plus a CSS subset plus inline SVG to PDF, with no I/O
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-render-has-no-io` through `rule:core-classes/pdf-output-is-inert`), on the class
surface `rule:core-classes/pdf-roster` names and the four exports of `wit/pdf.wit`. The engine is our own
cascade, layout and pagination over `krilla`, `parley`, `harfrust` and `taffy`
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-one-engine`), with `html5ever` shared with `Core\Html`
(`rule:core-classes/html-parsing`); the pagination layer is the largest item. The subset is data a test
reads (`rule:core-classes/pdf-css-subset-table`), and the rest of the contract is its own rules: tables
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-tables-across-pages`), the per-page header and footer function
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-header-is-a-page-function`), the standards and passwords
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-standards-and-passwords`), Factur-X
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-factur-x-is-the-one-attachment`), reading and merging existing PDFs
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-reader-and-merge`) and the Liberation fonts under OFL-1.1
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-default-fonts`).

**Part 3 — `Novis\Spreadsheet`.** The fourth built-in component, which never touches disk
(`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-has-no-io`), on the classes `rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-roster`
names and the resources of `wit/spreadsheet.wit`. Writing is a stream to the target the workbook was
created for (`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-writes-as-a-stream`), with the value tables of
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-value-types`, styled by default
(`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-styles-and-defaults`), the sheet-end parts and the chart subset
(`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-sheet-end-parts`), and formula injection closed by type
(`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-formula-is-a-value`). Reading is a stream of byte ranges for xlsx and
xlsb (`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-reads-as-a-stream`), template fill is
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-template-fill`, and CSV goes through the same classes. The only limits
are Excel's (`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-bulk-boundary`) and the decompression bound. Writing ods
and an explicit evaluate call are `rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-evaluation-and-roster`'s second wave
and outside this acceptance.

**Verify:** Part 2 is verified by the checks of the goal `pdf`. For Part 1, an AVIF and a JPEG XL fixture
with an orientation tag and an embedded profile decode upright and in sRGB, `Image::info` returns the
`exif` fields a fixture carries, and an animated fixture of each animated format decodes every frame with
`frames` equal to its count.
Part 3 is verified by the checks of the goal `spreadsheet`.
