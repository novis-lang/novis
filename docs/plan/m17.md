# M17 — Document components: the image second wave, `nvs/pdf` and `nvs/spreadsheet` (not yet sized)

**Backlog 6.** The goal `pdf` carries Parts 1 and 2 as one goal, and Part 3 is unscheduled. Its one
precondition is M9, which is done: the image component and the extension system that loads, caps and
pins a component are on disk. Every part is a first-party component built into the `nvs` binary
(`rule:packaging/the-first-party-components-are-built-in`), always on, under `Novis\`.

**The line against M9.** M9 builds the image component's first wave as the extension system's proof, and
keeps it. This milestone is everything `rule:core-classes/image-format-roster`, `rule:core-classes/pdf-render-has-no-io`,
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-has-no-io` and `rule:core-classes/pdf-page-is-an-image-source` placed and contracted but left unscheduled:
three parts, each large enough to be one or more goals of its own.

**Part 1 — the image component's second wave.** SVG and PDF join
`rule:core-classes/image-format-roster` decode only, through `resvg` and `hayro`, with `Image::open`
taking a `page` and a `dpi` and no new entry point (`rule:packaging/pdf-decoding-ships-in-the-image-package`).
`rule:core-classes/pdf-page-is-an-image-source` and `rule:core-classes/pdf-decode-refusals` are the PDF
contract. Two prerequisites gate linking `hayro`, per the first of those rules: it builds for the
component's `wasm32` target, and its dependency tree passes `deny.toml`. There is no pdfium fallback,
because pdfium in wasm needs Emscripten and has no WASI path. `text(...)` shapes every script through
`harfrust`. Barcodes are `rule:core-classes/image-barcode-roster`: the 1D codes and DataMatrix as SVG or
raster, `QrCode::svg` and the GiroCode helper, behind the `barcode` export of `wit/image.wit`.

**Part 2 — `Novis\Pdf`.** HTML plus a CSS subset plus inline SVG to PDF, with no I/O
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-render-has-no-io` through `rule:core-classes/pdf-output-is-inert`), on the class
surface `rule:core-classes/pdf-roster` names and the four exports of `wit/pdf.wit`. The engine is our own
cascade, layout and pagination over `krilla`, `parley`, `harfrust` and `taffy`
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-one-engine`), with `html5ever` shared with `Core\Html`
(`rule:core-classes/html-parsing`); the pagination layer is the largest item. The subset is data a test
reads (`rule:core-classes/pdf-css-subset-table`), and the rest of the contract is its own rules: tables
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-tables-across-pages`), the per-page header and footer function
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-header-is-a-page-function`), the standards and passwords
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-standards-and-passwords`), Factur-X
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-factur-x-is-the-one-attachment`), reading and merging existing PDFs
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-reader-and-merge`) and the Liberation fonts under OFL-1.1
(`rule:core-classes/pdf-default-fonts`).

**Part 3 — `nvs/spreadsheet`.** Reading and writing under one registered class, with formula injection
closed by type (`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-has-no-io` through
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-output-is-inert`). The roster is
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-evaluation-and-roster`'s first wave: xlsx reads and writes at parity,
xlsm reads data only, the binary and legacy formats read, ods reads, and template fill is named. The write
path offers the bounded-memory streaming mode `rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-bulk-boundary` requires. Owed
here: the builder's API, and the cell cap's knob, default and config block that rule leaves to the
building milestone. Writing ods and the explicit evaluate call are
`rule:core-classes/spreadsheet-evaluation-and-roster`'s second wave; the evaluate call waits on IronCalc
1.0 and is outside this acceptance.

**Verify:** Parts 1 and 2 are verified by the checks of the goal `pdf`, which are the acceptance of both.
For Part 3, the fixtures the five `spreadsheet-*` rules owe, each asserting inside the guest, a manifest
that requests no capability, and a million-row streaming export measured with peak memory proportional
to rows in flight rather than rows total.
