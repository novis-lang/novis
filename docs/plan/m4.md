# M4 — Language completeness — a usable CLI language (~10 weeks)

**Its loop goal was reached** — every check in that goal's acceptance list passes, which is
what closed the language holes. The *Verify* paragraph's **1000 `.nvst` cases** are met too: that is a
corpus figure rather than a feature gate, and the parity program's goals carried the tree well past it —
`bun nv disk` counts what is there now. Nothing here is open.

Full ordered-hash arrays with COW — including `Throwable`'s `backtrace` member, carried over from M3 and
landing as `array<string>` (`crates/nvs-types/src/error_lib.rs:334`) — `uint` arithmetic, and the
conversion operator over every row of
`rule:types/declaration`'s conversion table; exceptions propagating correctly by
checked return across JIT frames (`rule:errors/propagation`), closures that bind `$this`
only where the body uses it (`rule:statements/static-is-a-member-modifier`),
inheritance/interfaces, including default/private interface method bodies and `by`-delegation
(`rule:classes/no-traits`), object ordering through
`Comparable` (`rule:classes/comparable`), enums
(`rule:enums/closed-integer-type`), `decimal` arithmetic
(`rule:types/decimal`), generators and `foreach` over the two iteration interfaces
(`rule:iteration/two-interfaces`),
by-reference parameters (`inout`), instance members and static members including late static binding
(`static::`, `new static()`, `: static`), property hooks and `PropertyObserver`
(`rule:classes/property-observer`), `clone`
(`rule:classes/two-copy-depths`), `Core\Debug::dump` and its plaintext
rendering over the one record model (`rule:errors/diagnostic-record`)
— with a `secret`-qualified property's value redacted
(`rule:security/secret-qualifier`) — `Stringable` and the rest of
`rule:classes/no-magic-methods`, and `#[...]` attribute syntax on every
declaration it attaches to, including the explicit `<T>` call-site type argument
`Core\Attributes::get<T>`/`::all<T>` need even though their retrieval body doesn't land until M8
(`rule:attributes/inert-metadata`).

Also in this milestone: `nvs test` and the `.nvst` format — deliberately a **superset of `.phpt`'s
sections**, so the M11 importer is mechanical rather than a rewrite. Both are built;
[`crates/nvs-test`](../../crates/nvs-test/src/lib.rs)'s module doc is the one home for every section, what
`--EXPECTF--`'s escapes match, and why a case runs in a subprocess. What is left here is the corpus, and
the **Verify** list below is what it has to cover.

**Distinct from that, and landing at this milestone's tail with M4S:** the testing capability Novis *programs*
use — `#[Test]` and the table the compiler builds from it, `#[Fixture]`, `#[TestWith]`, the generic
`Core\Test` assertion roster, the ledger behind a catchable failure, and the human/JUnit/JSON reporters.
`rule:testing/test-attribute` owns all of it, including § 24's milestone table for
the pieces that land later; `.nvst` and `#[Test]` answer different questions and are never unified (§ 23).

**Verify:** hand-written conformance suite ≥ 1000 `.nvst` cases green, including `uint` at `0`, `i64::MAX`,
`i64::MAX + 1` and `2^64 − 1`; every conversion in `rule:types/declaration` both succeeding and throwing; overflow throwing
rather than promoting to `float`; key order preserved across insert, delete, re-insert and every sort
function; `Core\Arr::keys` typed `array<string>`; `Core\Json::encode` output identical to PHP's
`json_encode` under `JSON_UNESCAPED_SLASHES | JSON_UNESCAPED_UNICODE` for both lists and maps
(`tests/differential/core/json-decode-matches-json_decode-and-json_last_error.nvst`). `new static()` through two levels of inheritance returns the called class, and a closure written in a
method without naming `$this` is unbound — `bindTo()` on it rebinds nothing, which is `rule:statements/static-is-a-member-modifier`'s single
divergence and gets its own case. Plus one fixture per rule in the *Verification* section of ADRs
[0014](../decisions/0014.md), [0023](../decisions/0023.md),
[0028](../decisions/0028.md),
[0046](../decisions/0046.md) and
[0069](../decisions/0069.md) — each of those sections is the one home for
what its own rule requires, including the "a class that does not implement `PropertyObserver` shows no
measurable overhead" measurement and the diagnostic `$a + $b`/`$a += $b` over two arrays now owes. A non-trivial program runs correctly and leaks nothing under Valgrind — the
*argument-parsing, file-processing* half of that program moves to M8 with `Core\Cli` (§ 15) and `Core\IO`
(§ 14), since neither argv nor a file handle is reachable before capabilities exist at M6.
