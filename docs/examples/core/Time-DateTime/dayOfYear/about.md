Returns the number of the day within its year, where 1 January is day 1.

The result is from 1 to 365, or from 1 to 366 in a leap year. 1 February is day 32. After February
the number depends on the year: 1 March is day 60 in 2023 and day 61 in 2024.

Use it to count the days that have passed in a year, or the days that are left. It is also a simple
way to pick one item from a list for each day, such as a tip of the day.

**Good to know:** the count starts at 1.
