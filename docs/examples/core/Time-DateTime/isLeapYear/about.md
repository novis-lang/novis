Checks whether the year of a date and time is a leap year.

The result is `true` when the year has a 29 February, so it has 366 days. In other years the result
is `false`, and the year has 365 days.

A year that divides by 4 is a leap year. A year that divides by 100 is not, unless it also divides
by 400. So 2024 and 2000 are leap years, and 1900 and 2100 are not.

Use it when a calculation needs the number of days in the year, for example to split a yearly price
into a price per day.
